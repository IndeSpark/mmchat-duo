Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

MMChat Duo is made by IndeSpark, LLC. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

We don’t collect your data

MMChat Duo has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. IndeSpark, LLC doesn’t keep your messages, contacts, account details or usage data. Push notifications are described below.

Where your information goes

The app connects only to the Mattermost server you sign in to. Your messages, files and account live on that server and are governed by its operator’s policies.

Images and link previews in messages are provided by your server. An image someone posts from another website may be loaded from that website.

Push notifications

Push notifications work on Mattermost servers set up for MMChat Duo. On those, the server sends each notification through MMChat Duo’s push relay, which runs alongside it, to Apple’s Push Notification service for delivery to your device. A notification holds what your server puts in it: who it’s from, the channel, and the message text if the administrator chose to include it.

The relay doesn’t store notifications or their contents. It keeps short operational logs of whether each delivery succeeded, without message text. Your device’s notification token is kept by your Mattermost server with your session and removed when you sign out. On other servers, the app doesn’t receive push notifications.

Supporter Pass and your free channel

The Supporter Pass is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and tells the app only that the pass was bought and when; IndeSpark, LLC receives no payment or contact details from the app.

The free channel you choose is saved as a preference in your account on your Mattermost server, so it’s the same on all your devices. Your server’s operator can see it.

What stays on your device

  • Your session: kept in the Keychain so you stay signed in.
  • Your server address and settings: kept in the app’s preferences.
  • Unsent drafts: kept in the app’s own storage, protected until you first unlock your device after a restart, and deleted after 30 days.
  • Photos and files: the app reads only the ones you choose to attach, and sends them to your server.

Signing out removes your session, settings and drafts from the device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

MMChat Duo isn’t directed to children. Any accounts are managed by your Mattermost server’s operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
