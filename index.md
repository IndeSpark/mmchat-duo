# MMChat Duo

A native client for Mattermost servers, made for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Mac.

## Getting started

1. Open MMChat Duo and enter your server's address (for example `chat.example.com`).
2. Sign in with your Mattermost username or email and password.
3. If your account uses two-step verification, enter the code from your authenticator app. Codes can fill in automatically from Passwords.

You need an account on a Mattermost server. MMChat Duo doesn't create servers or accounts.

## Questions

**Which servers work?**
Mattermost servers reachable over HTTPS. Sign-in with single sign-on (SAML, OpenID, GitLab and similar) isn't supported yet.

**Why don't I see Add Members or Make Channel Admin?**
They follow your server's permissions. If your role can't manage a channel's members, the app doesn't offer it.

**Where are my messages stored?**
On your Mattermost server. See the [privacy policy](privacy/).
